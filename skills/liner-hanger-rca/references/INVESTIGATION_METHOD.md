# Investigation Method
1. Define incident, equipment and document revisions; check if retrospective, and escalate any active hazard.
2. Make evidence register with provenance and data quality; preserve original files.
3. Normalize telemetry units/timezones, document gaps and calibration; connect strokes, pump-rate and volumes only when pump factors are verified.
4. Build job-specific expected stages from engineer-approved programme and OEM documents.
5. Reconstruct actual timeline with signal-context, depth, equipment position and annotations.
6. Identify earliest supported departure in either procedure or telemetry; it may precede reported failure.
7. Compare at least two credible mechanism hypotheses; list evidence for, evidence against, and discriminating tests.
8. Keep tool state inference distinct from the directly observed surface signals.
9. Obtain qualified engineer feedback, classify conclusion, retain uncertainties and review actions.
10. Store verified learning as a linked case with pointers back to the original source evidence.
