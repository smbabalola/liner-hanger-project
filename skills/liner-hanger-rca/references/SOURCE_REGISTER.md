## Usage rules
- This register establishes **engineering background**, not authority to set/override limits.
- A manufacturer claims a feature for its specific product; do not assume another product behaves the same.
- If a source is unavailable or appears revised, mark it for recheck; do not fabricate exact specifications.
- Publicly accessible does not mean permission to redistribute full PDFs, diagrams, training notes or copyrighted manuals. Retain links and short original summaries, seek permission for licensed content.
