# Minimum Data Intake
Job: ID, well alias, operator/tenant, time zone, rig logger clock, tool maker/model/revision, operation dates, validity of permission.
Equipment: workstring and BHA/liner tally with lengths, OD, component positions and event-dependent connection states.
Procedure: approved version, operation stage sequence, source page, setpoint/limits and units, any approved exceptions.
Surface log: timestamp, MD or string/bit depth, hookload, SPP, pump rate, strokes, cumulative pumped volume, RPM, torque; pit/returns/flowout if supplied.
Context: fluid density/rheology and changes, well trajectory and casing sizes where available, expected drag/hydraulics if specifically computed and approved.
Evidence: original filename or URL, creator, record timestamp, calibration, sample frequency, gaps, quality flags and any reviewer notes.
Excluded by default: wireline and formation-evaluation logs.
